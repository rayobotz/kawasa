kawasa
More understanding, less confusion.

From zero, to capable.

Kawasa is a free web app for learning the Japanese people actually speak every day — at home, in shops, at the station, at work. Not textbook Japanese, and not exam Japanese: the real, polite, everyday kind.

Meet Kanchiru-kun

Your guide is a mousedeer — in Indonesian folktales, the smallest animal in the forest, famous for outsmarting crocodiles and tigers with his wits. He is a fellow beginner, not a teacher: he starts from zero, and his Japanese grows together with yours.

What's inside

  • Step-by-step units for real situations — introducing yourself, shopping, and more — each with a one-minute watch-first clip, real voices, and gentle practice.
  • Phrase of the Day — one everyday expression a day, explained through how people really use it, with examples and the reply you will actually hear back.
  • Short stories you can listen to and tap word-by-word, plus short articles on how Japanese communication really works.
  • A full kana path — hiragana and katakana with stroke-by-stroke animation and sound.

One honest note

Kawasa was made for Indonesians who dream of living and working in Japan, so the explanations inside are in Indonesian — that is the whole point of the app. You are warmly welcome to explore it anyway. Here is a small map so you don't get lost:
